Traceability: from farm to finished product

Every step is a control point, from partner producer to store shelf.

  1. Step 1

    Partner producer

    Animals of known origin, with selected genetics and technical guidance from our team.

  2. Step 2

    Transport and arrival

    Our own fleet and low-stress handling on arrival, with every lot recorded.

  3. Step 3

    Slaughter and processing

    A modern line, careful trimming and SIF oversight throughout the process.

  4. Step 4

    Quality control

    Testing in accredited laboratories and lot identification on every package.

  5. Step 5

    Finished product

    Shipped in a refrigerated fleet, with each lot traceable back to its farm of origin.
