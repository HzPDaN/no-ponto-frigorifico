Home/Quality and safety

Quality is not a differentiator. It is a daily obligation.

Production built on the strictest industrial quality standards, with guaranteed food safety and product integrity.

Five commitments, in this order

  1. Origin

    A trained team that sources the best animals, with selected genetics and guaranteed origin. We partner with producers, supporting them with information and guidance.

  2. Traceability

    100% traceable animals, from partner producer to finished product.

  3. Process excellence

    A trained team, strictly followed processes and technology that optimizes production, with special attention to trimming.

  4. Food safety

    A SIF inspector supports and monitors every step from receiving to finished product, with ongoing testing in accredited laboratories.

  5. Product excellence

    Fresh meat with a high level of fat finish and characteristics that deliver superior tenderness, flavor and juiciness.
